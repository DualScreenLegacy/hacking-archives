The Modern Golden Age of 3DS Modding

Why the scene is safer and more accessible than ever

With the official closure of the Nintendo eShop in March 2023, homebrew and custom firmware have become essential for game preservation, localization patches, save management, and customizing themes. Thanks to foolproof universal guides like 3ds.hacks.guide, almost any 3DS family system (Original 3DS, 2DS, New 3DS, New 2DS XL) can be safely modified using an SD card and a modern exploit in under 30 minutes without hardware soldering.
